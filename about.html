The SIG

About SIG-ILSP

The Special Interest Group on Indian Language Speech Processing is one of the Special Interest Groups of the International Speech Communication Association (ISCA). It connects people working on speech and spoken language technology for the languages of India, in academia, industry and government.

What this site is for

  • Outcomes: papers, PhD theses, and resources such as datasets, benchmarks, models and toolkits.
  • Lectures: a monthly online lecture series, recorded and archived here.
  • People and news: who works on what, and what is happening.

How to contribute

  • Add your paper: fill in the paper form. It appears on the site once a maintainer approves it.
  • Add a dataset or resource: fill in the resource form.
  • Add a PhD thesis: fill in the thesis form (authors or supervisors).
  • Join the people directory: fill in the directory form. You appear on the People page and map, placed by district.
  • List your group: fill in the group form.
  • Anything else (an event, a speaker, a correction): open an issue.

The forms need a free GitHub account. Everything on the site is stored as simple data files, so maintainers do not need web development experience or any special tools.

Hosting

This site is hosted for the SIG by SPIN Lab, IIT Guwahati, and is maintained by SIG volunteers.
